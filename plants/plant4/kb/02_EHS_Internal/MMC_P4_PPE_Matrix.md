# MMC Plant 4 — Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Matrix
**Document ID:** MMC_P4_PPE_Matrix  
**Document Owner:** EHS Manager, Plant 4  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-26  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and visitors at MMC Plant 4

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the minimum Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) requirements for all work areas and tasks at MMC Plant 4. It provides a matrix of required PPE by location, task-specific requirements, and procedures for PPE selection, use, inspection, and replacement.

---

## 2. Scope

This matrix applies to:
- All production areas: Line 1 (Stamping & Forming), Line 2 (Assembly & Robot Cells), Line 3 (Conveyor & Pack-Out)
- All shared areas: Receiving/Shipping, Maintenance Crib, Chemical Storage Cage, Battery Handling Station, Tool Calibration Room
- All personnel: employees, contractors, temporary workers, and visitors
- All shifts: 1st, 2nd, and 3rd shift operations

---

## 3. Regulatory Reference

This program complies with:
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.132** — General Requirements for PPE
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.133** — Eye and Face Protection
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.135** — Head Protection
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.136** — Foot Protection
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.137** — Electrical Protective Equipment
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.138** — Hand Protection
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95** — Occupational Noise Exposure

## 4. Minimum PPE Requirements — Plantwide

The following PPE is required for **all personnel** upon entering the production floor:

| PPE Type | Specification | Standard |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Safety Glasses | ANSI Z87.1+ rated, side shields required | ANSI Z87.1 |
| Safety Footwear | Steel or composite toe, slip-resistant sole | ASTM F2413 |
| High-Visibility Vest | Class 2 minimum in forklift traffic areas | ANSI/ISEA 107 |

**Exceptions:**
- Visitors escorted through designated walkways only: safety glasses and closed-toe shoes minimum
- Office personnel in EHS Office and Training Area: standard business attire acceptable within designated zones

## 5. Area-Specific PPE Matrix

### 5.1 Line 1 — Stamping & Forming

| Hazard | Required PPE | Specification |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| Noise (>85 dBA) | Hearing protection | NRR 25+ earplugs or earmuffs |
| Oil mist | Safety glasses with anti-fog coating | ANSI Z87.1+ |
| Pinch points / sharp edges | Cut-resistant gloves | ANSI A4 minimum |
| Flying debris / ejected material | Face shield (over safety glasses) | ANSI Z87.1+ |
| Heavy material handling | Metatarsal guards | ASTM F2413 Mt |
| Press operation proximity | Long sleeves prohibited; no loose clothing | Plant policy |

**Additional Requirements:**
- Hearing protection is **mandatory** at all times within the Line 1 boundary
- Face shields required when within 10 feet of active press operations
- Metatarsal guards required for coil handling and die change activities

---

### 5.2 Line 2 — Assembly & Robot Cells

| Hazard | Required PPE | Specification |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| Pinch points | Cut-resistant gloves | ANSI A3 minimum |
| Unexpected robot motion | Safety glasses | ANSI Z87.1+ |
| Torque tool operation | Impact-resistant gloves | EN 388:2016 |
| Electrical troubleshooting | Electrical-rated gloves + leather protectors | ASTM D120, Class 00 minimum |
| Static-sensitive components | ESD wrist strap or heel grounders | ANSI/ESD S20.20 |

**Additional Requirements:**
- No loose clothing, jewelry, or lanyards near robot cells
- Long hair must be secured and contained
- ESD protection required when handling electronic subassemblies

## 7. PPE Selection Procedure

### 7.1 Hazard Assessment
1. EHS Manager conducts hazard assessments for each work area and task
2. Assessments are documented and retained per OSHA requirements
3. Reassessment occurs when:
   - New equipment or processes are introduced
   - An incident indicates inadequate protection
   - Employee concern is raised
   - Annual review cycle

### 7.2 PPE Selection Criteria
PPE is selected based on:
- Nature and severity of hazard
- Conditions of exposure (duration, frequency, concentration)
- Fit and comfort for extended wear
- Compatibility with other required PPE
- Regulatory and customer requirements

### 7.3 Approval Process
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| EHS Manager | Approves all PPE specifications and substitutions |
| Supervisor | Ensures correct PPE is available and used |
| Purchasing | Procures only EHS-approved PPE items |

**Substitutions:** No PPE substitutions without prior EHS Manager approval. Equivalent or better protection must be demonstrated.

## 8. PPE Issuance and Replacement

### 8.1 Initial Issuance
| PPE Item | Issued By | Quantity |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Safety glasses | EHS / Supervisor | 1 pair + 1 spare |
| Hearing protection (reusable) | EHS / Supervisor | 1 set |
| Cut-resistant gloves | Supervisor / Maintenance Crib | 2 pairs |
| High-visibility vest | EHS / Supervisor | 1 vest |
| Hard hat (if required) | EHS / Supervisor | 1 unit |
| Specialty PPE (electrical, chemical) | EHS | Per task assignment |

### 8.2 Replacement Criteria
PPE shall be replaced when:
- Damaged, worn, or defective
- Contaminated and cannot be decontaminated
- Expired (per manufacturer guidelines)
- No longer fits properly
- After any incident involving PPE contact

### 8.3 Replacement Procedure
1. Employee notifies Supervisor of PPE replacement need
2. Supervisor verifies condition and authorizes replacement
3. Old PPE is returned (if reusable type) or disposed of properly
4. Replacement issued from Maintenance Crib or EHS stock
5. Specialty items (electrical gloves, respirators) require EHS coordination

## 9. PPE Inspection Requirements

### 9.1 User Inspection (Before Each Use)
All employees must inspect PPE before each use:

| PPE Type | Inspection Points |
|----------|-------------------|
| Safety glasses | Scratches, cracks, loose frames, damaged side shields |
| Hearing protection | Cracks, hardening, contamination, seal integrity |
| Gloves | Cuts, tears, punctures, chemical degradation, loss of grip |
| Hard hat | Cracks, dents, UV degradation, suspension damage |
| High-visibility vest | Tears, fading, reflective tape condition |
| Safety footwear | Sole wear, toe cap exposure, lace/strap condition |
| Electrical gloves | Cracks, holes, embedded objects, ozone damage |
| Respirator | Seal condition, valve function, cartridge expiration |

### 9.2 Formal Inspection Schedule

| PPE Type | Inspection Frequency | Performed By |
|----------|---------------------|--------------|
| Electrical gloves | Before each use + every 6 months (dielectric test) | User / Certified lab |
| Respirators | Before each use + monthly fit check | User / EHS |
| Fall protection (if used) | Before each use + annual formal inspection | User / Competent person |
| Arc flash PPE | Before each use + annual inspection | User / EHS |

### 9.3 Defective PPE
- **Do not use** defective PPE under any circumstances
- Tag defective items "OUT OF SERVICE"
- Notify Supervisor immediately
- Obtain replacement before resuming work

## 10. PPE Training Requirements

### 10.1 Initial Training
All employees receive PPE training upon hire, covering:
- When PPE is required
- What PPE is required for their work area and tasks
- How to properly don, doff, adjust, and wear PPE
- Limitations of PPE
- Proper care, maintenance, and storage
- Useful life and replacement criteria
- How to request replacement PPE

### 10.2 Refresher Training
- Annual refresher for all employees
- Retraining when:
  - New PPE types are introduced
  - Employee demonstrates improper use
  - Workplace changes affect PPE requirements

### 10.3 Specialty PPE Training
| PPE Type | Training Requirement |
|----------|---------------------|
| Respiratory protection | Initial fit test + annual fit test + training |
| Electrical protective equipment | Qualified electrical worker training |
| Arc flash PPE | NFPA 70E awareness + task-specific training |
| Chemical protective equipment | HazCom + chemical-specific training |

## 12. Enforcement and Non-Compliance

### 12.1 Compliance Verification
- Supervisors verify PPE compliance during pre-shift meetings
- EHS conducts random PPE audits weekly
- Non-compliance observations are documented

### 12.2 Progressive Discipline
| Occurrence | Action |
|------------|--------|
| First | Verbal warning and immediate correction; documented |
| Second | Written warning; retraining required |
| Third | Final written warning; management review |
| Fourth or willful violation | Termination consideration |

### 12.3 Positive Recognition
Employees demonstrating consistent PPE compliance and proactive hazard reporting may be recognized through the plant safety recognition program.

---

## 13. PPE Storage and Care

### 13.1 Storage Requirements
- Store PPE in clean, dry location away from direct sunlight
- Hang or store to prevent deformation
- Keep away from chemicals, heat sources, and sharp objects
- Dedicated storage for contaminated PPE awaiting cleaning/disposal

### 13.2 Cleaning and Maintenance
| PPE Type | Cleaning Method |
|----------|-----------------|
| Safety glasses | Mild soap and water; lens cleaning wipes |
| Reusable hearing protection | Mild soap and water; air dry |
| Cut-resistant gloves | Per manufacturer instructions; replace if contaminated |
| Hard hat | Mild soap and water; do not use solvents |
| High-visibility vest | Machine wash per label; replace if faded |
| Respirator | Disassemble; clean per manufacturer; sanitize; air dry |

### 13.3 Disposal
- Dispose of contaminated PPE per waste handling procedures
- Chemically contaminated PPE may require hazardous waste disposal
- Cut or deface worn-out PPE before disposal to prevent reuse
