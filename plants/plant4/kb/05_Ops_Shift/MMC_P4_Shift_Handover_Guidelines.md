# MMC Plant 4 — Shift Handover Guidelines
**Document ID:** MMC_P4_Shift_Handover_Guidelines  
**Document Owner:** Plant Manager, Plant 4  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-26  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Applies To:** All supervisors, operators, maintenance personnel, and support staff at MMC Plant 4

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standardized shift handover procedures for MMC Plant 4. Effective shift handovers ensure continuity of operations, transfer of critical safety information, and clear communication of equipment status, quality issues, and pending actions between shifts.

---

## 2. Scope

This guideline applies to:
- All three shifts (1st, 2nd, 3rd)
- All production areas: Line 1, Line 2, Line 3
- All shared areas: Receiving/Shipping, Maintenance, Chemical Storage, Battery Handling Station
- All roles: Supervisors, Operators, Maintenance Technicians, Quality personnel

**Shift Schedule:**

| Shift | Hours | Days |
|-------|-------|------|
| 1st Shift | 06:00 – 14:00 | Monday – Friday |
| 2nd Shift | 14:00 – 22:00 | Monday – Friday |
| 3rd Shift | 22:00 – 06:00 | Monday – Friday |
| Weekend Maintenance | 06:00 – 18:00 | Saturday – Sunday (as scheduled) |

---

## 3. Shift Handover Principles

Effective handovers are based on the following principles:

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Face-to-Face** | Verbal handover between outgoing and incoming personnel |
| **Documented** | Written record of key information in handover log |
| **Complete** | All critical information transferred; no assumptions |
| **Verified** | Incoming personnel confirm understanding |
| **Timely** | Handover occurs during overlap period, not after |
| **Two-Way** | Incoming personnel ask questions; outgoing personnel clarify |

## 4. Handover Timing

### 4.1 Standard Handover Windows

| Transition | Time Window | Duration |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| 1st → 2nd Shift | 13:45 – 14:15 | 30 minutes |
| 2nd → 3rd Shift | 21:45 – 22:15 | 30 minutes |
| 3rd → 1st Shift | 05:45 – 06:15 | 30 minutes |

### 4.2 Handover Sequence

| Time (Relative to Shift End) | Activity |
|------------------------------|----------|
| -15 minutes | Outgoing personnel complete handover log entries |
| -10 minutes | Incoming personnel arrive at workstation |
| -5 to +10 minutes | Face-to-face handover at workstation |
| +10 to +15 minutes | Incoming personnel walk area; verify conditions |
| +15 minutes | Outgoing personnel released |

## 5. Supervisor Shift Handover

### 5.1 Supervisor Handover Requirements

The outgoing Line Supervisor must transfer the following information to the incoming Line Supervisor:

| Category | Information Required |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Safety** | Incidents, near-misses, hazards observed, open safety actions |
| **Personnel** | Attendance, injuries, personnel issues, visitors/contractors on-site |
| **Production** | Current job status, schedule changes, production counts, targets |
| **Quality** | Quality holds, defects, NCRs, inspection requirements |
| **Equipment** | Downtime events, equipment issues, pending repairs, LOTO status |
| **Maintenance** | Active work orders, scheduled PMs, contractor activities |
| **Materials** | Material shortages, incoming shipments, inventory issues |
| **Actions** | Pending decisions, escalations, follow-up items |

### 5.2 Supervisor Handover Procedure

| Step | Action | Responsibility |
|------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Complete shift production report | Outgoing Supervisor |
| 2 | Document all safety events and observations | Outgoing Supervisor |
| 3 | Update equipment status in downtime log | Outgoing Supervisor |
| 4 | Note any quality holds or issues | Outgoing Supervisor |
| 5 | Meet incoming Supervisor at designated handover area | Both Supervisors |
| 6 | Review handover log entries verbally | Outgoing Supervisor |
| 7 | Walk production floor together (time permitting) | Both Supervisors |
| 8 | Incoming Supervisor asks clarifying questions | Incoming Supervisor |
| 9 | Incoming Supervisor signs handover log acknowledging receipt | Incoming Supervisor |
| 10 | Incoming Supervisor briefs their team | Incoming Supervisor |

### 5.3 Supervisor Handover Checklist

**Safety:**
- [ ] Any incidents or near-misses this shift?
- [ ] Any new hazards identified?
- [ ] Any safety actions pending or overdue?
- [ ] Any personnel working under restrictions?
- [ ] Any active LOTO in progress?

**Production:**
- [ ] Current job and schedule status?
- [ ] Any schedule changes or priorities?
- [ ] Production counts vs. target?
- [ ] Any changeovers required next shift?

**Quality:**
- [ ] Any quality holds or suspect material?
- [ ] Any defects or NCRs issued?
- [ ] Any customer concerns communicated?
- [ ] Any special inspection requirements?

**Equipment:**
- [ ] Any equipment down or degraded?
- [ ] Any repairs in progress?
- [ ] Any Maintenance activities affecting production?
- [ ] Expected restoration times?

**Personnel:**
- [ ] Any absences or coverage issues?
- [ ] Any new personnel requiring support?
- [ ] Any visitors or contractors on-site?
- [ ] Any personnel issues requiring follow-up?

**Other:**
- [ ] Any material shortages?
- [ ] Any incoming shipments expected?
- [ ] Any escalations pending?
- [ ] Any other information incoming shift needs?

## 6. Operator Shift Handover

### 6.1 Operator Handover Requirements

Each outgoing Operator must transfer the following to the incoming Operator:

| Category | Information Required |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Equipment Status** | Current operating mode, any alarms or faults, abnormal conditions |
| **Production** | Current job, count, remaining quantity, any issues |
| **Quality** | Any defects, adjustments made, inspection results |
| **Safety** | Hazards observed, near-misses, PPE issues |
| **Housekeeping** | Area condition, cleanup needed, material placement |
| **Pending Actions** | Anything left incomplete, Maintenance requests |

### 6.2 Operator Handover Procedure

| Step | Action | Responsibility |
|------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Complete operator log entries for the shift | Outgoing Operator |
| 2 | Ensure workstation is in safe, orderly condition | Outgoing Operator |
| 3 | Meet incoming Operator at workstation | Both Operators |
| 4 | Walk the workstation together | Both Operators |
| 5 | Point out any abnormal conditions or concerns | Outgoing Operator |
| 6 | Review current job status and any quality issues | Outgoing Operator |
| 7 | Incoming Operator verifies equipment status | Incoming Operator |
| 8 | Incoming Operator acknowledges handover verbally | Incoming Operator |
| 9 | Outgoing Operator available for questions until released | Outgoing Operator |

### 6.3 Operator Handover — Line-Specific Items

#### Line 1 — Stamping & Forming

| Item | Handover Information |
|------|---------------------|
| Press status | Operating mode, stroke count, any faults or alarms |
| Die condition | Any die issues, adjustments made, hits since last PM |
| Coil status | Material remaining, coil changes pending, any feed issues |
| Scrap | Scrap rate, any scrap conveyor issues, bin status |
| Lubrication | Auto-lube system status, any manual lubrication performed |
| Noise/vibration | Any unusual sounds or vibrations observed |
| Hydraulics | Oil level, temperature, any leaks observed |

#### Line 2 — Assembly & Robot Cells

| Item | Handover Information |
|------|---------------------|
| Robot status | Operating mode, any faults, cycle time, error codes |
| Cell conditions | Any parts in cell, teach pendant location, E-stop status |
| Tooling | Socket condition, gripper condition, any changes made |
| Vision system | Inspection results, any false rejects, calibration status |
| Guarding | Guard status, any interlocks bypassed (should be none) |
| Part supply | Feeder status, parts remaining, any feed issues |

#### Line 3 — Conveyor & Pack-Out

| Item | Handover Information |
|------|---------------------|
| Conveyor status | All zones running, any jams cleared, any alarms |
| Packaging | Materials remaining (boxes, labels, wrap), any issues |
| Labeling | Label stock, print quality, any misapplications |
| Palletizer | Pallet count, any gripper issues, pattern running |
| Housekeeping | Floor condition, debris, shrink wrap scraps |
| Ergonomics | Any discomfort, rotation schedule status |

## 7. Maintenance Shift Handover

### 7.1 Maintenance Handover Requirements

Outgoing Maintenance Technicians must transfer the following:

| Category | Information Required |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Active Work** | Work orders in progress, status, expected completion |
| **LOTO Status** | Any active lockouts, equipment affected, expected duration |
| **Parts** | Parts on order, parts used, parts needed |
| **Equipment Issues** | Known problems, troubleshooting performed, findings |
| **PMs** | PMs completed, PMs due, any deferrals |
| **Contractors** | Contractor work in progress, escort requirements |
| **Safety** | Any safety concerns, near-misses, hazards discovered |

### 7.2 Maintenance Handover Procedure

| Step | Action | Responsibility |
|------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Update work order status in CMMS | Outgoing Technician |
| 2 | Document active LOTO status on LOTO log | Outgoing Technician |
| 3 | Note any parts used or needed | Outgoing Technician |
| 4 | Complete Maintenance handover log | Outgoing Technician |
| 5 | Meet incoming Technician at Maintenance Crib | Both Technicians |
| 6 | Review active work and equipment status | Outgoing Technician |
| 7 | Walk to any active job sites together | Both Technicians |
| 8 | Transfer LOTO responsibilities per LOTO SOP | Both Technicians |
| 9 | Incoming Technician signs handover log | Incoming Technician |
| 10 | Incoming Technician briefs Maintenance Supervisor | Incoming Technician |

### 7.3 LOTO Transfer During Shift Change

When lockout extends across shifts, the following procedure applies:

| Step | Action | Responsibility |
|------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Outgoing Technician verifies work status and equipment condition | Outgoing Technician |
| 2 | Outgoing Technician briefs incoming Technician on lockout scope | Outgoing Technician |
| 3 | Incoming Technician verifies zero energy state | Incoming Technician |
| 4 | Incoming Technician applies personal lock to lockbox/isolation points | Incoming Technician |
| 5 | Outgoing Technician removes personal lock | Outgoing Technician |
| 6 | Transfer documented in LOTO log | Both Technicians |
| 7 | Maintenance Supervisor notified of transfer | Incoming Technician |

**Reference:** MMC_P4_LOTO_SOP Section 10.2 — Shift Transfer During Group Lockout

## 11. Abnormal Situation Handover

### 11.1 Extended Downtime

When equipment is down and expected to remain down across shifts:

| Requirement | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| Document root cause and status | Include in handover log |
| Identify expected restoration time | Communicate to incoming shift |
| Note parts ordered or needed | Update in handover log and CMMS |
| Communicate production impact | Notify incoming Supervisor |
| Brief Maintenance on status | Maintenance handover required |

### 11.2 Active Emergency

If an emergency is in progress during shift change:

| Requirement | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| Incoming personnel report to muster point | Do not assume normal duties |
| Outgoing personnel remain on scene as directed | Support emergency response |
| Handover deferred until emergency resolved | Document as soon as practical |
| Incoming Supervisor briefs incoming team | Once all-clear given |
| Incident debriefing conducted | Before resuming normal operations |

### 11.3 Active Incident Investigation

When an incident investigation is in progress:

| Requirement | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| Scene preservation | Do not disturb evidence area |
| Witness availability | Outgoing witnesses may be held for statements |
| Documentation transfer | All notes transferred to investigation lead |
| Area restrictions | Communicate restricted areas to incoming shift |
| Investigation status | Update incoming Supervisor |

### 11.4 Customer Audit or Visit

When a customer or auditor is on-site:

| Requirement | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| Visitor notification | Inform incoming shift of visitors |
| Escort requirements | Transfer escort responsibility if visitor spans shifts |
| Audit scope | Communicate areas being audited |
| Document requests | Transfer pending requests |
| Special requirements | Communicate any customer-specific requirements |

| Method | When Used | Documentation |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Face-to-face verbal | All handovers | Supported by written log |
| Written handover log | All handovers | Required |
| Workstation walk | All handovers | Visual verification |

### 12.2 Supplemental Methods

| Method | When Used |
|--------|-----------|
| Phone/radio | When personnel cannot meet in person (exception only) |
| Email | Supervisor-to-Supervisor for extended documentation |
| CMMS notes | Maintenance work order updates |
| Production system | Supervisor shift reports |

### 12.3 Remote/Phone Handover (Exception Only)

Phone handovers are permitted only when:
- Personnel are in different locations and cannot meet
- Approved by Supervisor or Maintenance Supervisor
- Written documentation completed immediately after call
- Incoming personnel verify conditions upon arrival

## 13. Handover Failures and Escalation

### 13.1 Common Handover Failures

| Failure | Example | Impact |
|---------|---------|--------|
| No handover | Outgoing leaves without handover | Critical information lost |
| Incomplete handover | Safety events not communicated | Repeat hazard exposure |
| No documentation | Verbal only, no written log | No record for investigation |
| Late handover | Handover after incoming shift starts work | Information missed |
| Assumed information | "Everything normal" without details | Hidden issues continue |

### 13.2 Consequences of Handover Failures

| Failure Type | Consequence |
|--------------|-------------|
| First occurrence | Verbal coaching and reminder |
| Repeated occurrence | Written warning; documented in personnel file |
| Failure resulting in incident | Investigation and corrective action |
| Willful neglect | Disciplinary action up to termination |

### 13.3 Escalation Procedure

| Condition | Action | Escalate To |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Outgoing personnel leaves without handover | Incoming Supervisor contacts outgoing by phone | Shift Supervisor |
| Critical safety information not transferred | Investigate and document gap | EHS Manager |
| Equipment issue not communicated causing damage | Investigate root cause | Maintenance Supervisor |
| Quality issue not communicated causing escape | Investigate and contain | Quality Manager |
| Pattern of handover failures | Review and corrective action | Plant Manager |

## 15. Handover Audit and Verification

### 15.1 Audit Schedule

| Audit Type | Frequency | Performed By |
|------------|-----------|--------------|
| Handover log review | Weekly | Line Supervisor |
| LOTO transfer verification | Per transfer | Maintenance Supervisor |
| Safety content audit | Monthly | EHS Manager |
| Handover observation | Quarterly | Plant Manager or designee |

### 15.2 Audit Criteria

| Element | Verification |
|---------|--------------|
| Log completeness | All fields completed; both signatures present |
| Timeliness | Handover occurred during overlap window |
| Safety content | Safety events and hazards documented |
| Face-to-face | Evidence of verbal handover (witness, observation) |
| Follow-through | Actions from handover completed or transferred |

### 15.3 Corrective Actions

Handover deficiencies are addressed through:
1. Coaching and feedback to individuals
2. Reinforcement at shift briefings
3. Refresher training if pattern observed
4. Process improvement if systemic issues identified
5. Disciplinary action for repeated willful non-compliance

## Appendix D: Critical Handover Items Quick Reference

**NEVER leave without communicating:**

| Category | Must Communicate |
|----------|-----------------|
| 🔴 **Safety** | Any incident, near-miss, or new hazard |
| 🔴 **LOTO** | Any active lockout — MUST transfer properly |
| 🔴 **Quality Hold** | Any material on hold — location and status |
| 🟡 **Equipment Down** | Any equipment not running — cause and ETA |
| 🟡 **Pending Actions** | Anything requiring follow-up next shift |
| 🟢 **Normal Status** | Confirm if everything is normal |

**If in doubt, write it down and talk it out.**
