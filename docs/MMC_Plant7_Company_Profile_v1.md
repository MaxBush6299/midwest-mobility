## 1. Company Overview

**Midwest Mobility Components (MMC)** is a mid-sized Tier 1/Tier 2 manufacturer supplying subassemblies and critical components to the mobility industry, including:
- Electric vehicle (EV) OEMs
- Heavy equipment manufacturers
- Commercial fleet and mobility providers

MMC specializes in **mechanical assemblies, electrified subcomponents, and safety-critical parts** used in propulsion, braking, and structural systems.

---

## 2. Plant 7 Overview

**Location:** Illinois, USA  
**Employees:** ~280  
**Operating Model:** 3-shift, 24/5 with weekend maintenance  
**Plant Type:** High-mix / medium-volume manufacturing

Plant 7 includes:
- Automated and semi-automated production lines
- Robotic assembly cells
- Manual pack-out and kitting areas
- Chemical handling for cleaning and bonding
- Battery component handling (EV programs)
- Internal logistics using forklifts and pallet jacks

---

## 3. Production Lines & Areas

### Line 1 — Stamping & Forming
- High-tonnage presses
- Coil-fed material handling
- Guarded but high-energy equipment
- Common hazards: pinch points, ejected material, noise, oil mist

### Line 2 — Assembly & Robot Cells
- Collaborative and traditional industrial robots
- Servo-driven torque tools
- Vision inspection systems
- Common hazards: unexpected motion, bypassed guarding, PPE noncompliance

### Line 3 — Conveyor & Pack-Out
- Powered roller conveyors
- Manual pack-out stations
- Stretch-wrap and labeling systems
- Common hazards: jams, pinch points, housekeeping, ergonomic strain

---

### Shared Areas
- Receiving / Shipping (forklift traffic)
- Maintenance Crib
- Chemical Storage Cage
- Battery Handling Station (EV components)
- Tool Calibration Room
- EHS Office and Training Area

## 4. Safety & Compliance Context

MMC operates under:
- **OSHA general industry standards (29 CFR 1910)**
- State-level safety requirements
- Customer-specific safety clauses
- Internal EHS management system aligned to **ISO 45001 principles**

Plant 7 uses:
- Plant-specific SOPs for LOTO, guarding, PPE, HazCom, and forklift safety
- Visual inspections and audits as part of daily operations
- Incident, near-miss, and corrective action tracking
- Annual refresher training for all operators

---

## 5. Quality Management Context

MMC Plant 7 operates under a quality system aligned to **ISO 9001 principles**, including:
- Defined standard work and changeover procedures
- Nonconformance reporting (NCR)
- Corrective and preventive action (CAPA / 8D)
- Internal audits
- Supplier traceability for safety-critical components

---

## 6. Maintenance & Reliability Context

Maintenance is a mix of:
- Preventive maintenance (PM) on fixed schedules
- Condition-based maintenance on critical assets
- Reactive maintenance for breakdowns

Key systems:
- Conveyor Line 3 (highest downtime)
- Robot cells on Line 2 (most safety risk)
- Presses on Line 1 (highest energy risk)

Maintenance activities frequently require:
- Lockout/Tagout
- Guard removal and verification
- Temporary safe states during troubleshooting
